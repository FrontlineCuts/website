Summary

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics tools and loads no third-party content. Fonts and videos are hosted on the same server as the website. Videos only load when you play them. Personal data is processed when you visit the pages (server log files at the hosting provider), when you email us and when we apply to one of your job or project postings. Details and our service providers are listed below.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, the provider processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, the referring page and information about your browser and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of the website. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. According to GitHub, such transfers are based on appropriate safeguards, in particular the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact by email

When you email us, we process your details (for example name, email address, company and the content of your message) to answer your request and, where applicable, to prepare or carry out an order. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for other requests. Our mailbox is operated via iCloud Mail by Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. Incoming messages are also forwarded to a Gmail mailbox of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which we also use to receive notifications from job and project platforms.

We delete your request once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. We keep business letters and invoices for the periods required by German commercial and tax law.

Applications to postings and business contact

When we apply to a public job posting, project request or call for collaboration, we process the business contact details published there (for example company, name and role of the contact person, business email address and phone number), the content of the posting and our correspondence with you. The data comes from publicly accessible sources, in particular job boards, project and freelance platforms and company websites.

The purpose is the application, preparing an order and following up on it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your posting invites applications, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is winning orders. We manage these contacts in an internal contact list. If we receive no reply, we delete your data no later than six months after the last contact. If a business relationship develops, the statutory retention periods apply.

You can object at any time, without giving reasons, to the processing of your data for direct marketing (Art. 21(2) and (3) GDPR), for example with an informal email to business@frontlinecuts.de. We will then no longer contact you and delete your other data. So that we can respect your objection permanently, we keep only your email address or domain, the company, the date and the scope of the objection in a suppression list (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR) for as long as the objection applies.

If we contact you through a platform (for example freelancermap, freelance.de, join.com, Interlance, Upwork or Malt), the platform operator also processes your data under its own responsibility and privacy policy.

Footage you send us for orders

We use videos and other files you provide for an order exclusively to carry out that order. We only publish work as a reference with your express consent. We process the material on our own devices and, where applicable, with the service providers named below. On request, we agree with you in advance which services are used for your order. After the order is completed, we delete the material once it is no longer needed for corrections, warranty or an agreed archive.

Service providers and recipients

In addition to the providers already named, Apple (email), Google (email forwarding and, in individual cases, providing files via Google Drive) and GitHub (hosting), we use AI-based assistance services of Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Ireland (Claude). We use them to organise our communication and contact list, to prepare replies and applications and to support video editing. The content of your messages, your contact details and files you provide may be processed in the process. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the efficient organisation of our business.

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

Transfers to third countries

The providers named may also process data in the USA or other countries outside the EU. According to their information, they base such transfers on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (in particular the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies) or on EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We also work remotely, currently from Türkiye. When we access your data from there, we do so only as far as necessary for the purposes described.

External links

This website contains links to external sites, for example the licence pages of Pexels and Mixkit. Data is only transferred to the respective provider when you click such a link. Their privacy policy applies there.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object to the processing on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal email to us is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Postfach 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany.
