How it works

Four steps, fully remote. We work in English or German, by email or video call. Our working hours (UTC+3) overlap with the European business day and with US East Coast mornings.

  1. Request

    Tell us briefly what you need. We clarify the goal, platforms and footage, and confirm scope and price in writing.

  2. Footage and brief

    You send raw footage or existing ads via link, plus product information and your brand guidelines.

  3. Edit

    Within three business days you receive all variations for review.

  4. Revision and delivery

    We apply your feedback in one bundled round. Then you receive the final files.
